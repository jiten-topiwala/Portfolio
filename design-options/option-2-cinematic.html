— Chapter One

A practice built on first principles, refined through field deployment.

— Plate I — in studio.

Trained as a mechanical engineer, evolved into a roboticist by necessity — every machine I've built has demanded both the discipline of the drafting board and the agility of software.

The work spans surveillance robots that fold for transport, pipe inspection systems that survive immersion, and agricultural rovers that navigate uneven fields without complaint.

A great machine is not the one with the most parts. It is the one that disappears into its purpose.

Currently building autonomous systems for sub-20HP agricultural vehicles, while founding LiftTerra — a startup tackling construction and maintenance through robotics.
