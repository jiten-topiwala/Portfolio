Decision matrix — narrowing 9 to 1

All nine are professional, all nine have great motion. The real question is what personality you want the site to project.

  • Want minimal but motion-rich (the 2026 standard) → Option 07 Kinetic Type, 08 Liquid Glass, or 09 Swiss. These are what awwwards-tier portfolios look like today.
  • Want maximum range & timelessness → Option 02 Cinematic, 05 Magazine, or 09 Swiss. Will age well, work for any audience.
  • Want to be unforgettable → Option 04 Mission Control, 06 Mecha, or 03 Bento. High screenshot-rate.
  • Want maximum engineering credibility → Option 01 Blueprint or 04 Mission Control. Tech audiences will love both.
  • Want premium soft modern → Option 08 Liquid Glass. Most "Apple Vision Pro" of the set.
  • Want designer's-designer minimalism → Option 09 Swiss or 07 Kinetic Type. Most sophisticated.
  • Hybrids work — pick a primary + a secondary, e.g., Swiss landing + Cinematic case-study pages, or Glass landing + Kinetic project pages. Once you commit, I'll build the full site with your real content.
