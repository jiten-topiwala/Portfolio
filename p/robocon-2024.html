MathWorks Award

Robocon 2024 — Harvest Day

Two collaborative robots for ball shooting and seedling placement

₹70K total prize money · 3rd in league among 56 national teams

Team / Sub-group Lead · competition · 2023-09

Overview

Participated in Robocon 2024 under the 'Harvest Day' theme. Challenge involved designing two collaborative robots: a semi-autonomous robot that picked and shot coloured balls and placed seedling-like poles, and a fully autonomous robot that navigated the field, detected coloured balls, and placed them into matching baskets without any human intervention. Contributed to the mechanical design of custom shooting and seedling-handling mechanisms in Fusion 360 and SolidWorks; integrated IMUs, colour sensors, and proximity sensors. Developed autonomous navigation and control algorithms using ROS2, Gazebo, Python, and MATLAB; performed extensive simulation and testing for precise coordination. Led a group within the team.

What it does

  • Semi-autonomous shooter robot with custom seedling-handling mechanism
  • Fully autonomous robot with colour-based ball detection and basket placement
  • ROS2 + Gazebo simulation for control validation
  • Fusion 360 / SolidWorks mechanical design
  • Sensor fusion: IMU, colour sensors, proximity sensors
