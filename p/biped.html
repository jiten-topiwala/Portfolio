Biped — Dynamic Walking Robot

Stable bipedal walker with inverse kinematics + RL gait optimisation

15% weight reduction · improved stability, energy efficiency, and terrain adaptability

Designer / Builder · research · 2025-01

Overview

Designed and prototyped a stable biped robot, integrating lightweight mechanical structures with real-time control systems for dynamic walking. Implemented inverse kinematics and feedback control on embedded systems, and explored gait optimisation using MATLAB simulations and reinforcement learning. Reduced weight by 15% and improved overall stability, energy efficiency, and terrain adaptability through iterative mechanical and control-system refinement.

What it does

  • Lightweight mechanical structure (~15% weight reduction through iteration)
  • Inverse kinematics + feedback control on embedded hardware
  • Gait optimisation via MATLAB simulations and reinforcement learning
  • Improved stability, energy efficiency, and terrain adaptability
  • Real-time control loop
