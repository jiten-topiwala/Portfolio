Agricultural Gantry System

Rope-driven farm-coverage robot

Published in IJIRT (Vol. 11, Issue 4)

Lead Author / Designer · research · 2023-06

Overview

Developed a gantry-based agricultural robotic system for farm coverage with strong use-case advantages over both traditional and other advanced systems. Robot moves along one axis on a rope (attached to a cable winch for adjustment) and on two base drives along the perpendicular axis. Carries an arm for various operations, a tank, and appropriate sensors for navigation and applications. Base drives at the field ends also act as water-refill, charging, and storage points. Published as: 'Design of an Agricultural Robotic System for Farm Coverage', IJIRT, Vol. 11, Issue 4, pp. 109–115 (2024).

What it does

  • Rope + cable-winch primary axis with two base drives on perpendicular axis
  • Arm, tank, and sensor payload for farm operations
  • Endpoint base drives as water-refill / charging / storage stations
  • Published research validating the architecture
