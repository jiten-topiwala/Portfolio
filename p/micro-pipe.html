Miniature Pipe Inspection Robot (35–95 mm)

Gear-based transformable miniature inspector — ATtiny85 control

35–95 mm pipe diameter · ATtiny85-based feedback controller, PCB designed in EasyEDA

Research Intern · research · 2024-05

Overview

Conceptual design of a miniature pipe-inspection robot for 35–95 mm diameter pipes using a gear-based transformation mechanism. Did analysis and simulation, then designed a feedback controller for the miniature unit under tight component-size constraints. Studied multiple control methods, designed the conceptual layout and PCB schematic in EasyEDA using an ATtiny85 microcontroller, and forwarded it for validation and manufacturing — focusing on compactness and control precision.

What it does

  • Gear-based transformation for 35–95 mm pipe diameters
  • Feedback controller designed under tight size constraints
  • PCB schematic in EasyEDA with ATtiny85 microcontroller
