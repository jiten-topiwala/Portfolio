LiftTerra

Revolutionising Construction & Maintenance

50 kg+ payload · autonomous navigation & follow-me mode

Co-founder / Robotics · robotics

Overview

LiftTerra is a robotic platform designed to automate heavy lifting and material transport on construction sites. By reducing physical strain on workers and increasing efficiency, LiftTerra aims to modernise the construction industry. Features heavy payload capacity (50 kg+), autonomous navigation in cluttered environments, follow-me mode for worker assistance, robust outdoor chassis, and long battery life.

What it does

  • Autonomous navigation in cluttered environments
