Pipe Inspection Robot (400–900 mm)

Variable-diameter inspection robot — IIT Roorkee research

Pipe diameters 400–900 mm · CFD-validated thrust design

Research Intern · research · 2024-05

Overview

Researched many pipe-inspection-robot architectures, then designed a versatile system capable of seamless navigation and inspection of pipelines with varying characteristics, diameters (400–900 mm), and fluid dynamics. Conceptual design and analysis/simulation of a scissor-mechanism robotic module. Control system used Raspberry Pi as navigator, Xbox controller as remote operator; ArduSub + QGroundControl + BlueOS + Cockpit for vehicle setup, configuration, and navigation. Tried multiple actuator-control techniques including sign-magnitude PWM (Cytron driver), locked anti-phase PWM (brushed ESC), relays, and direct MOSFET drive. Tested individual and integrated components. Did MATLAB modelling and CFD analysis to optimise motion dynamics and thrust force requirements during design verification. Improved inspection efficiency and reduced manual intervention.

What it does

  • Scissor-mechanism diameter adjustment (400–900 mm)
  • Thruster, BLDC, and brushed-motor actuator integration
  • Raspberry Pi + ArduSub + QGroundControl + BlueOS stack
  • MATLAB modelling + CFD analysis for thrust optimisation
  • Multi-strategy motor-driver evaluation (PWM modes, relays, MOSFETs)
